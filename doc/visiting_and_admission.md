# CityCare Hospital – Visiting Hours and Admission Procedure

## Visiting hours
- General wards: 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM daily, and 11:00 AM to 12:00 noon on Sundays.
- Private rooms: 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily.
- ICU and NICU: 11:00 AM to 11:30 AM and 5:00 PM to 5:30 PM. Only 1 visitor at a time, no children under 12.
- Maximum 2 visitors per patient at a time in general wards. Visitors must wear a visitor pass at the entrance.
- Flowers and outside food are not permitted in ICU, NICU, or oncology wards.

## Outpatient (OPD) registration
1. Register at the front desk or online at the hospital portal to get a UHID (Unique Health ID).
2. Pay the consultation fee at the billing counter.
3. Wait for your token number to be called. OPD registration is open 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
Carry a government photo ID and previous medical records.

## Inpatient admission
1. Admission is initiated by a doctor's admission advice, or through Emergency.
2. Submit ID proof, insurance card (if any), and the admission advice at the Admission Desk (Main Lobby, open 24x7).
3. A refundable advance deposit is collected: Rs. 10,000 for general ward, Rs. 25,000 for private room, Rs. 50,000 for ICU.
4. The patient is allotted a bed and a wristband with UHID.

## Discharge
Discharge is processed by 11:00 AM on the day of discharge. The final bill is prepared 2 hours before discharge. Patients receive a discharge summary, medicines, and follow-up appointment date.
