# CityCare Hospital – Facilities, Insurance and Contact

## Facilities
- 350 beds in total, including 40 ICU beds and 12 NICU beds.
- 24x7 pharmacy in the Main Lobby.
- Diagnostic lab (blood tests, pathology): open 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Reports are usually ready within 24 hours; emergency reports within 2 hours.
- Radiology: X-ray, CT scan, MRI, ultrasound. MRI and CT require prior appointment.
- Cafeteria on the Ground Floor, open 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- Parking: free for 2 hours for visitors, paid after that. Wheelchairs available at every entrance.
- Free Wi-Fi in waiting areas and private rooms.

## Health checkup packages
- Basic Health Check (blood sugar, lipid profile, ECG, CBC): Rs. 1,999.
- Executive Health Check (adds chest X-ray, ultrasound abdomen, thyroid, kidney and liver function): Rs. 4,999.
- Senior Citizen Package (adds bone density and echocardiography): Rs. 6,499.
Fasting for 10 to 12 hours is required. Book at least one day ahead.

## Insurance and payment
- Cashless treatment is available with most major insurers and TPAs. Carry your insurance card and photo ID.
- Pre-authorization for planned admissions should be requested at least 48 hours in advance at the Insurance Desk.
- Payments accepted: cash, credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking.
